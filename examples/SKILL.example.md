# Aesthetic Profile — Acme — warm editorial

This taste profile favors clearly high typographic contrast with large display type, a polished, refined finish, expressive, characterful display/serif type, a strict, aligned grid and a light, bright canvas. Lean into the spirit of: Editorial Serif, Editorial Serif · Bold, Luxury Dark, Editorial Serif · Dark. Steer away from: Vivid Maximal · Dark, Vivid Maximal, Neo-Brutalist · Cyan, Dark Techno.

## Design principles

- **Type contrast → Dramatic** (+56): high typographic contrast with large display type.
- **Finish → Refined** (-41): a polished, refined finish.
- **Type voice → Expressive** (+35): expressive, characterful display/serif type. _(exploratory)_
- **Layout → Structured** (-25): a strict, aligned grid.
- **Tone → Light** (-22): a light, bright canvas. _(exploratory)_
