# Aesthetic Profile — Acme — warm editorial

This taste profile favors clearly high typographic contrast with large display type, expressive, characterful display/serif type, a polished, refined finish, a light, bright canvas and a strict, aligned grid. Lean into the spirit of: Editorial Serif, Editorial Serif · Dark, Editorial Serif · Bold, Bauhaus Primary. Steer away from: Vivid Maximal · Cool, Dark Techno, Vivid Maximal · Dark, Soft Minimal · Dark.

## Design principles

- **Type voice → Expressive** (+41): expressive, characterful display/serif type — **key driver**.
- **Tone → Light** (-26): a light, bright canvas — **key driver**.
- **Depth → Flat** (-2): flat surfaces with little elevation — **key driver**.
- **Saturation → Muted** (-15): a muted, desaturated palette. _(exploratory)_
- **Layout → Structured** (-21): a strict, aligned grid.
- **Temperature → Warm** (+17): warm color temperature.
- **Geometry → Geometric** (-14): geometric, hard-edged shapes. _(exploratory)_
- **Finish → Refined** (-37): a polished, refined finish.
- **Type contrast → Dramatic** (+57): high typographic contrast with large display type.
